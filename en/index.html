Lin Sun

Lin Sun is a senior algorithm researcher and foundation-model team lead at 360. His research and engineering work spans foundation models, post-training, LLM safety, trustworthy generation, RAG, memory, and agent systems.

Since 2010, he has worked in AI research and management at Tencent, 360, Beike, and Rushi, across recommendation, search and question answering, data intelligence, and AI applications. His responsibilities span research problems, technical direction, model training, systems engineering, and cross-functional team leadership.

01 / Research

Research & engineering

Foundation models & post-training

Research on long-context data selection, reasoning-path supervision, and model merging to improve reasoning efficiency and generalization.

Safety & trustworthy generation

Research on dynamic attacks, safety alignment, and hierarchical policy control, balancing risk protection, task utility, and generation reliability.

RAG, memory & agents

Research on evidence loss in complex documents, multi-document evidence allocation, long-term memory, and experience transfer in realistic agent workflows.
