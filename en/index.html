RESEARCH · ENGINEERING · LEADERSHIP

Turning foundation-model capability into work people can trust and use.

Lin Sun is a senior algorithm researcher and foundation-model team lead at 360. His work spans foundation models, post-training, LLM safety, RAG, memory, and agent systems.

I have worked across Tencent, 360, Beike and Rushi on recommendation, search and question answering, data intelligence, foundation models, and LLM safety. I currently lead a foundation-model team at 360, connecting research, engineering, team building, and production delivery. Recent work appears across NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP, AAAI, ECAI, and ICASSP, with a continuing focus on LLM safety, RAG, memory, and agent systems. Related team work has received the Beijing Science and Technology Progress Award and top results in SafetyAI Bench and SuperCLUE-RAG.
