Research / Project detail

RAG & trustworthy knowledge services

Led the InduOCRBench study and paper, establishing industrial-document OCR-RAG evaluation across 570 documents from 12 industries.

RAGDocument AIEvidence reasoning
Problem

Retrieval and OCR quality alone do not guarantee reliable answers. How can systems allocate evidence across documents and account for document noise?

Personal role

Owned the research question, evaluation system, experiment organization, result analysis, and InduOCRBench paper writing, alongside the RAG technical direction, system design, and delivery.

Method

Combining retrieval, document structure, evidence allocation, and generation evaluation. Related papers cover OCR robustness for RAG, budgeted multi-document evidence allocation, and visual-document RAG.

Results

InduOCRBench covers 570 documents, 3,402 pages, 12 industries, and 11 challenge types. In the paper experiments, OCR accuracy for visual-style documents was 82.9%, while downstream RAG accuracy was 52.8%.
