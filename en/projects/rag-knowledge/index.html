Research / Project detail

RAG & trustworthy knowledge services

From visual documents and evidence allocation to reliable answers in complex information workflows.

RAG Document AI Evidence reasoning
Problem / motivation

Retrieval and OCR quality alone do not guarantee reliable answers. How can systems allocate evidence across documents and account for document noise?

Personal role

Technical direction, system design, evaluation, and delivery.

Approach

Combining retrieval, document structure, evidence allocation, and generation evaluation. Related papers cover OCR robustness for RAG, budgeted multi-document evidence allocation, and visual-document RAG.

Results

RAG research and the 360 Zhibrain team system.

Method and results

BEAR uses hierarchical semantic indices, coarse-to-fine evidence allocation, and localized recovery across nine evaluation settings. On DragonBall it reports 85.55% recall and 91.86% completeness with an approximately 4K-token final evidence context. The OCR paper covers 11 challenging document types and shows why character-level OCR scores can miss downstream RAG failures.
