Research / Project detail

LLM safety & trustworthy generation

Research and engineering connecting adversarial testing, safety alignment, and online governance.

AlignmentSafetyTrustworthy AI
Problem

How can protection against evolving jailbreaks, harmful content, and hallucinations preserve useful task performance?

Personal role

Second project member and technical lead for innovations 1 and 2; responsible for the overall design, key algorithms, model training, system design, and delivery.

Method

Attack generation → evaluation → safety training → online protection → experience feedback. Related work includes TriPlay-RL and Magic-Token safety control.

Results

Public work includes TriPlay-RL, Magic Token, detection–response separation, and trustworthy generation. The related project received the Beijing Science and Technology Progress Award, and a CAAI certificate records the achievement “Key Technologies and Applications of Large-Model Safety.”

Evidence

Papers: TriPlay-RL, Magic Token, and Beyond Static Alignment. Public evidence: the Beijing Science and Technology Progress Award and CAAI appraisal certificate 鉴字[2025]第008号 dated 2025-06-19.

Public boundary

Only public project, paper, and appraisal evidence is shown; internal production metrics remain outside the site.
