Research / Project detail

TinyR1: efficient reasoning models

Exploring compact reasoning models through branch-merge distillation.

DistillationReasoningOpen research
Problem

How can compact models retain strong reasoning ability while making the method accessible to other researchers?

Personal role

Model training and release.

Method

Branch-Merge Distillation combines branch-specific distillation with model merging to improve reasoning.

Results

In the paper experiments, mathematics improved from 72.6 to 78.1, coding from 57.2 to 61.6, and science from 62.1 to 65.0. Branch-Merge took 0.5 hours on four H800 GPUs versus 23 hours on 32 H800 GPUs for the retraining baseline. The 5% figure refers to parameter scale relative to DeepSeek-R1 671B, not performance.

Evidence

Public evidence: the paper, Hugging Face model page, and the Peking University Computer Science news item.

Public boundary

TinyR1-32B-Preview, later safety models, and separate media reports are kept as distinct records.
