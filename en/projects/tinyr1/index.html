TinyR1: reasoning, safety & model merging

Open 32B models spanning branch-merge distillation, safety control, and distribution-aware model merging for reasoning, instruction following, and safety.

DistillationReasoningOpen research
TinyR1-32B model ↗Paper ↗TinyR1-32B-Preview model ↗PKU team news ↗SCF-RKL paper ↗
Problem

How can compact models retain strong reasoning ability while making the method accessible to other researchers?

Method

Preview transfers capabilities through math, coding, and science branches followed by merging. TinyR1-32B uses Control Tokens for reasoning and safety; SCF-RKL controls merging interference through sparse complementary updates and reverse KL.

Outcomes

TinyR1 received coverage from the National Natural Science Foundation of China, reached the Hugging Face Trending Top 5, and exceeded 10,000 downloads. Preview uses 32B parameters, about 5% of DeepSeek-R1’s 671B, with an AIME 2024 score of 78.1 versus 79.8 for DeepSeek-R1.
