About me

Profile

His work has evolved from search, question answering, and recommendation systems to language intelligence, data intelligence, large models, and agents, with a focus on applying model capabilities to real-world problems. Since 2010, he has worked in R&D and management at Tencent, 360, Beike, and Rushi, spanning algorithm research, systems engineering, technical direction, cross-functional team building, and product delivery.

He is currently a senior algorithm researcher and foundation-model team lead in 360’s Zhibrain Division, working on LLM post-training, safety and trustworthy generation, RAG and document intelligence, and memory and experience mechanisms for agents.

04 / Contributions

Research & industry contributions

Publications & open models

His first-author or corresponding-author publications appear at NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP, and AAAI. Representative work includes InduOCRBench and BEAR for document RAG, TriPlay-RL and Magic Token for LLM safety alignment, and research on model merging, long-context training, and agent experience transfer. He led the training and release of the open reasoning model TinyR1.

Patents & standards

He holds granted invention patents in LLM safety and model optimization, knowledge bases and corpora, search and question answering, and intelligent interaction. He contributed to three national and industry standards covering large-model evaluation, knowledge-graph frameworks, and competency requirements for VR professionals, as well as the Report on Large Language Model Safety (2024).
