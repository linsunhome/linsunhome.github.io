About me

Profile

Lin Sun is a senior algorithm researcher and foundation-model team lead at 360. His research and engineering work spans foundation models, post-training, LLM safety, trustworthy generation, RAG, memory, and agent systems.

Since 2010, he has worked in AI research and management at Tencent, 360, Beike, and Rushi, across recommendation, search and question answering, data intelligence, and AI applications. His responsibilities span research problems, technical direction, model training, systems engineering, and cross-functional team leadership.
